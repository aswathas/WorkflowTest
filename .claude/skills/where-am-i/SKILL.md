# Where Am I

Orient. Don't teach — just report where they are, accurately.

## Gather

1. **Learning records** — `learning-records/NNNN-*.md`, read in order. Highest
   number is the demonstrated floor.
2. **Notes** — `NOTES.md` for preferences and open misconceptions.
3. **Mission** — `MISSION.md`.
4. **Catalog** — `CATALOG.md` to locate the next lesson after their floor.

## Report

Plain, four parts:

**Demonstrated.** The highest phase/lesson they have evidence for. Cite the
record. Be exact — this is a claim about what they proved, not what they read.

**Exposed but unproven.** Lessons covered without evidence of understanding.
These are not gaps you can see; they might be fine. Say that.

**Next.** The one lesson just above the floor, with its prerequisites checked.
If a prerequisite is unmet, name it and offer to ground it first.

**Open threads.** Misconceptions from `NOTES.md` that are due for revisiting, and
any unconfirmed items the learner flagged (e.g. a model name not yet verified).

## If there are no learning records

Say so plainly: no evidence yet, so no claim about their level. Offer to start at
Phase 00 and evaluate as you go. Don't guess from how long they've been reading.

## On every session start

If this skill runs at the start of a session, keep the report to three lines.
The learner asked a question; they didn't ask for an audit.
